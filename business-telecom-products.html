BUSINESS VOICE

PRI, SIP Trunk & Business Voice Solutions

Traditional and IP-based voice connectivity can support businesses with large calling requirements, existing PBX infrastructure and customer-facing communication teams.

01

PRI

Primary Rate Interface is a traditional digital voice connectivity solution used by businesses requiring multiple voice channels through a business telephone system.

PRI availability, channel configuration and provider support depend on the applicable telecom offering.

02

SIP Trunk

SIP Trunk provides IP-based business voice connectivity and can offer greater flexibility and scalability for organizations using IP-PBX systems.

Channel capacity, numbering, calling types and plan features vary by provider.

03

Business Voice Connectivity

Voice connectivity can be designed around existing PBX infrastructure, business calling requirements and future communication plans.

INTERNATIONAL NUMBERS

Local DIDs for Supported Countries

A Direct Inward Dialing number, commonly called a DID, can provide a business with a local telephone number in a supported destination.

Local DIDs can be useful for businesses that want customers in another country to have a familiar local number to contact.

Number availability and requirements depend on the destination country, number type and applicable service plan.
